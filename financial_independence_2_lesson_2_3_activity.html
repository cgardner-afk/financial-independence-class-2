Part 3 — Bond Yield Comparison

Three hypothetical bonds each have a $1,000 face value, a 5% annual coupon, and five years remaining. Only their purchase prices differ. Compare current yield and yield to maturity (YTM).

BondPurchase priceAnnual couponCurrent yieldEstimated YTM

YTM is calculated numerically from five annual $50 coupon payments and a $1,000 principal repayment. It assumes the issuer makes every promised payment; realized compounded returns also depend on reinvestment rates.

Part 4 — Bond Risk Decision Lab

For each situation, choose the most suitable of the three hypothetical options. There may be tradeoffs, but one option best matches the stated goal.
