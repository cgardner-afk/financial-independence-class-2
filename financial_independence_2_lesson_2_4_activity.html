Part 3 — Investment Fee Simulator

Two hypothetical funds hold the same investments and earn the same return before expenses. Change the assumptions to see how their fees affect the ending balances.

Simplified model: annual gross return minus annual expense ratio, divided into monthly periods; contributions occur at month-end. Assumes constant rates and no taxes or additional fees. Real markets fluctuate, and future returns are not guaranteed.
